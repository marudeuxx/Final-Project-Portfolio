BSIT student — builds with C++, moves with BCP AllStar

Hi, I'm Renz, a BSIT student at Bestlink College of the Philippines. C++ is what I'm most comfortable with. I like writing my own functions and figuring out the logic myself instead of asking AI to do it. With a few of my classmates, I built a counter and ordering system that has e-wallet payment and some other features. When I'm not coding, I'm dancing with BCP AllStar, the talent center organization in our school.

What I work with

C++ is where I'm strongest. I can write functions on my own and work through the logic until I get it right. Building the ordering system with my classmates also taught me other things, like working with a team and making sure the payment part really works.
